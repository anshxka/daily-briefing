The UK's largest broadband provider, BT, has agreed to acquire rival operator TalkTalk in a rescue deal. The transaction will bring an end to months of uncertainty regarding TalkTalk's future and ensure that services for its 1.5 million retail customers and one million wholesale customers continue without disruption.

The deal will cost BT approximately £400 million, which covers the acquisition out of administration, associated fees, TalkTalk's projected £60 million loss for the year, and the writing off of £100 million owed to BT's Openreach.
