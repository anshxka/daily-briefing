Mistral AI Unveils Mistral Large 4 with a One-Trillion Parameter Model

French AI lab Mistral AI has released Mistral Large 4, a new large multimodal model known as "Le Chonk" due to its one trillion parameters. The model aims to compete with both American and Chinese rivals as an alternative to closed-source options.

Hark Launches Privacy-Focused AI Personal Assistant Hark Pro

Startup Hark has announced the wide release of Hark Pro, an AI personal assistant built around a foundation model trained specifically for computer use. Founded by serial entrepreneur Brett Adcock, the company offers a free tier alongside a subscription model for heavy users.

Hark Pro operates as a full-screen operating system experience featuring a central chat interface, proactive action prompts, and visual "panels" that act as mini-dashboards. Users configure the assistant to integrate with their digital lives—including email, calendars, and hard drives—enabling it to autonomously handle tasks like approving expenses, booking flights, or renewing car registrations.

A key user experience choice for Hark is displaying a small window showing the agent actively navigating the web, a transparency measure designed to build user trust. The company is positioning the product as a practical operating system layer for future AI-driven computers rather than pursuing artificial general intelligence.

Manager Availability Named as Biggest Predictor of L&D Success and Retention

A survey released on Sept. 25 by Wiley Workplace Intelligence reveals that the amount of time managers can spare to develop their people is one of the strongest indicators of employee retention and engagement. Surveying nearly 1,500 people, Wiley found that 84% of employees are very likely to stay with their companies when managers have time to develop direct reports, compared to only 41% when managers are unavailable. Engagement showed similar disparities, with 76% excited about company culture with an available manager versus 23% with an unavailable one.

Furthermore, employees ranked skills development and training at the top of their list for improving engagement, beating out mentorship, advancement transparency, and frequent manager conversations. In response, Wiley suggested that employers protect manager time, prevent burnout through capacity protection, and treat manager availability as a critical retention metric rather than a mere cultural nicety.

What this means for HR and business professionals: HR leaders must actively protect manager time and treat availability for employee development as a key measurable retention metric. Prioritizing structured skills training offers a cost-effective, high-impact strategy to boost organizational engagement without needing massive new budget investments.
