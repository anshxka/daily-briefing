Wipro Appoints Angan Guha to Head Americas 2 Strategic Market Unit

Indian IT company Wipro has appointed Angan Guha as president and CEO of its Americas 2 strategic market unit, a critical division handling clients in the US and Canada. Guha succeeds Suzanne Dann, who originally took the role in October 2022 and departed the company in May.

Guha rejoins Wipro following a four-year stint as the global CEO and managing director of Birlasoft. Before that, he spent over two decades at Wipro, holding roles such as CEO of the same Americas 2 business unit and serving on the executive leadership team. His return comes as the Americas 2 unit faces slowing revenue growth due to clients reining in discretionary spending amid macroeconomic and geopolitical challenges. Despite this, the unit contributed 25.6% to Wipro's revenue as of the quarter ended September 30, making it the company's third-biggest strategic market unit.

Guha will now be tasked with navigating these economic headwinds and stabilizing growth across the vital North American market segment.

Why it matters: Leadership transitions within key revenue-generating units highlight how organizations leverage seasoned executives to tackle macroeconomic pressures. Business and HR leaders must align workforce capabilities closely with shifting client demands during periods of slowed revenue growth.
