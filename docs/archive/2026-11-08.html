Under the provisions of the bill, employers planning workforce displacements due to AI integration will be required to provide affected employees with at least six months' advance notice.

Abu Dhabi Court Holds NMC Health Founder BR Shetty Liable for $5.4 Billion Fraud

The Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM) Court of First Instance has found NMC Health founder BR Shetty and former chief executive Prasanth Manghat liable for fraudulent and wrongful trading. Justice Sir Andrew Smith ruled that the starting assessment for financial contributions the two executives must make toward the company's assets stands at $5.397 billion, following the company's catastrophic collapse.

NMC Health was previously the largest private healthcare provider in the United Arab Emirates, managing an extensive network of hospitals, clinics, and day surgery centers across Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Sharjah, and Al Ain. The group entered administration in April 2020 after an investigation by short-seller Muddy Waters Research exposed widespread balance-sheet manipulation. Inquest findings revealed that company leadership diverted corporate funds for personal use and concealed substantial debts, accumulating approximately $3.88 billion in undisclosed borrowings against just $1.99 billion in reported obligations by late 2018.

The court formally rejected Shetty's defense that he was an unwitting victim of his subordinates, as well as Manghat's claim that he lacked financial control as the leader of a FTSE 100 enterprise. The court determined that both executives knowingly participated in fraudulent schemes dating back to April 2012, triggering direct liability under the ADGM Insolvency Regulations.

Why it matters: This landmark judgment underscores the critical necessity of rigorous internal financial controls and independent executive oversight to prevent catastrophic corporate governance failures. For HR and senior executives, it reinforces that top corporate officers face severe personal legal liability and cannot avoid accountability by claiming detachment from financial operations.
