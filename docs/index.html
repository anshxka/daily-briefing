ITC Reports 14% Rise in Employee Costs to Rs 1,887 Crore in Q2 FY27 Amid Profit Decline

Indian conglomerate ITC saw its employee benefit expenses increase by 14 percent year-on-year to Rs 1,886.52 crore in the second quarter of FY27, compared to Rs 1,654.49 crore in the same period last year. This represents an additional employee-related expenditure of approximately Rs 232 crore, occurring even as the company's consolidated net profit attributable to owners declined 18.7 percent to Rs 4,168.40 crore.

During the same quarter, ITC's consolidated net revenue increased 3.9 percent to Rs 20,269 crore, while EBITDA declined 16.8 percent to Rs 6,126.53 crore and operating profit margins contracted. The rise in employee costs comes as different segments experienced varied trajectories; the cigarette business faced pressure with a 26 percent decline in segment profit, while non-cigarette FMCG, paperboards, and agri businesses recorded revenue and profit growth.

The non-cigarette FMCG business achieved its fourth consecutive quarter of double-digit revenue growth, up 19.1 percent to Rs 7,218.36 crore. Meanwhile, the paperboards and packaging business reported a revenue increase of 35.9 percent, and the agri business grew revenue by 12.7 percent.
