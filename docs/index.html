SpaceX Achieves Historic Midair Catch of Starship During Orbital Test

SpaceX achieved a major aerospace breakthrough on Saturday by completing the first-ever midair recovery of its Starship spacecraft. Returning from orbit, the 171-foot-tall upper-stage craft descended through the clouds at the Starbase launch facility in Texas, reignited its engines to rotate vertically, and was caught by the launch tower's mechanical arms, known as "Mechazilla."

The spacecraft navigated over sparsely populated areas and executed a final maneuvering loop before landing.

SpaceX confirmed that the spacecraft's systems, including its heat shield, operated effectively throughout the test. Company officials stated that the successful catch demonstrates the full reusability of the Starship system, advancing the long-term goal of rapid, low-cost space recycling.
